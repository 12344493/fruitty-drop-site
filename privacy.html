Fruitty Drop Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Fruitty Drop is a puzzle game. We built it so that it doesn't collect any information about you.

Information we collect

None. Fruitty Drop does not collect, store, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking.

Game progress

Your scores, stars, and the fruit you've found are saved only on your device so you can pick up where you left off. They are never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes this progress.

Internet access

The game works fully offline and does not connect to the internet.

Children

Fruitty Drop is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no information, it collects none from children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
