How do I play?

Drag to aim and tap to drop a fruit into the jar. Two of the same fruit merge into a bigger one. Keep the jar below the dashed fill line. Tap Shake the Jar when the pile gets stuck.

What are the levels?

Tap the shop door to go inside the market. Each level has a goal and a limited number of drops; finishing with more drops left earns more stars.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, on your device. Deleting the app also deletes your progress.
